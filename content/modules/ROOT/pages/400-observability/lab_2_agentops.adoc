= Lab 3.2 — AgentOps: Tools, Traces and Evidence

Verify a price lookup using the actual catalogue evidence and model/tool spans.
The same prepared MCP tool is available in the OpenShift AI Playground.

== Follow one price check

In Card Scout, click *Check price* on an analyzed Pokémon card, then open its
*View MLflow trace* link. The link selects workspace `signal-desk-dev`, experiment
`card-scout` and that exact trace.

Open *Details & Timeline → Graph*. Expect `card.research`, `agent.responses` and
`tool.get_card_market_data`. Select *Inputs / Outputs*: verify the selected item,
exact catalogue ID, edition/number, rarity, provider currency, update timestamp
and returned prices. The model span records the visible response and reported
usage. Provider headers, keys and hidden reasoning are excluded.

Batch traces contain `cards.analyze_batch` and individual `d3.classify_card`
spans. Image hashes and the rubric are recorded; raw image bytes are omitted.
Inspect an uncertain or incorrect classification rather than assuming a high
probability guarantees correctness.

The app's numeric price fields come from the successful MCP result. A model
answer without a matching call is rejected. An agent may write an incorrect
sentence even after a tool call: compare its answer with evidence in the trace.
If a trace is absent, inspect MLflow permissions and exporter configuration;
a visible UI answer alone does not prove trace export succeeded.

The Playground uses its normal OpenShift session and selected MaaS subscription.
The public MCP connector requires no additional app login. Gen AI Playground and
external MaaS models are Technology Preview in OpenShift AI 3.5.

== Check behavior

[cols="2,3",options="header"]
|===
| Test | Expected behavior
| Rare and common cards from different eras | Predictions are distinct from held-out catalogue labels; unknowns remain visible.
| Yu-Gi-Oh!, receipt, Pokémon illustration | Classified as controls, with no Pokémon price button.
| Check a known Pokémon item | Actual tool call, exact catalogue reference, dated prices or explicit unavailability.
| D3 rarity differs from catalogue | Both values remain visible; the prediction is not silently rewritten.
| Unknown item or arbitrary URL | Rejected; no caller-selected URL, shell or purchase tool exists.
| Model or provider unavailable | Visible error and preserved progress; no invented result.
| Reload the app | Cached analysis and successful research remain available.
|===

Catalogue rarity does not establish market value, physical condition or
 authenticity. The fixed dataset knows its catalogue references; recognition
of arbitrary uploaded editions is outside this exercise.
