Sources & how it works

Live Bluesky posts → decision model → positive candidates → research agent → this feed.

D3 Mini classifies every eligible post as positive, negative, mixed, neutral or uncertain. Only positive candidates scoring at least 65% go to DeepSeek for source review. These scores are model outputs, not a guarantee of correctness.

“Source read” means the claim was checked against one fetched article. “Cross-checked” means the agent found support on multiple source domains; it does not establish that the reporting is independent. This is a POC, not a guarantee of truth. Unreadable or unsupported claims are held back.
