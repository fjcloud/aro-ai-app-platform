# Dataset attribution

English Base Set metadata and source card scans come from
[TCGdex](https://www.tcgdex.net/) and its
[card database](https://github.com/tcgdex/cards-database).
The manifests retain exact source URLs and SHA-256 checksums. All 102 scans are
bundled unchanged to make the workshop reproducible without image downloads.

The database project's MIT software licence does not relicense Pokémon artwork,
trademarks or marketplace data. Those remain with their respective rights
holders. These are existing source scans, not AI-generated or retouched cards.

Cardmarket and TCGplayer retain their own terms and data rights. Market values
are fetched on demand with provider timestamps. A catalogue aggregate is not a
verified 1st Edition quote, a sale offer or a grading/authenticity assessment.


The three unchanged negative-control images are attributed to:

- [PokeAPI: Pikachu official artwork](https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites/blob/master/sprites/pokemon/other/official-artwork/25.png).
- [YGOPRODeck: Blue-Eyes White Dragon](https://db.ygoprodeck.com/api/v7/cardinfo.php?id=89631139).
- [Scryfall: Black Lotus, Limited Edition Alpha](https://scryfall.com/card/lea/232/black-lotus).

These artworks retain their owners' rights. Controls are excluded from Pokémon
rarity and market-price lookups; no Magic or Yu-Gi-Oh! valuation is implied.
